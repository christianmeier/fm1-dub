Knobs, faders and wheels: the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down faster. Buttons, pads and keys: Space or Enter presses, held as long as you hold it; Shift with Space keeps a button held until you press it again. Pads and keys: the arrows move between them.

The SMK-37's controls

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob, a fader or a wheel up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it. The pitch wheel springs back when you let go.
  • Click or touch buttons, pads and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go.

Keyboard

  • Tab goes through the controls. The pads are one stop, the keys another: the arrows move along them.
  • Shortcuts, while the keyboard has the focus: pads 1–8 Q–I, 9–16 A–K; the black keys from F#3 1–0, -.
  • Space SEQ PLAY, Enter SEQ REC, Esc PATCH (HOME), Z / X OCTAVE− / +, ↑ ↓ K6 (SELECT).

Saving

SAVE (BT) keeps your pieces in this browser, apart from the FM-1 emulator's. Factory reset forgets them. The SMK-37's TRS jack is a MIDI output this build does not use; it has no key lights and no button glow.
