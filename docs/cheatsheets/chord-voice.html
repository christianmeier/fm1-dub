5 · The moving knob

On its page
The Target's knob (Cutoff or Reso here, Colour on the FM page) shows the LFO: its reach two pale lines either side of the ring, a white dot where it is now.

6 · Vary the Chord page's KNOB 4

A trim on Form's Vary: the chord's amount is Form's plus this. Centred: 0 the notch.
0
As Form's Vary (the pieces' own: 0 to -17).
Right
The stab pattern turns more often (every two bars it may move a step, gain or lose a stab); more throws.
Left
Stiller: fewer turns and throws, fewer stabs and bars left out (never more than Form's Vary leaves out).
HOLD
Far left (the word inside the ring): never, whatever Form says. Every stab of the pattern played, no throws but a fill's last.

7 · Its level, its strip OCT · SEL

OCT- / OCT+
On any chord page: its level, 5 a press. Both together: muted, or back.
The strip
SEL, white 1 (F3) held: the chord's Level, Echo, Plate, Filter (sheet 2).
ENV held
0.5 s, a nudge: Attack, Colour, Reso, Decay, Cutoff (an FM voice: its Ratio, Feedback) a step. 1.5 s, a shake: another voice (not Noise), every value a big step.
