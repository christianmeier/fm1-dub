2 · Vary and its trims each layer's part page

Each layer's own Vary is a trim on Form's: its amount is Form's Vary plus the trim, 0 to 100. Centred: 0 follows Form, far left HOLD, that layer never turns. At an amount of 0 (Vary 0, the trims 0; or Hold) nothing turns and no stab is thrown.
ChordENV
The stab pattern turns or gains or loses a stab (every two bars); stabs and whole bars drop out; the throws.
PingsARP
At a loop's end, a note moved or changed; a pass sat out.
BassLFO
The plan drawn anew (kept 2 to 6 phrases first); the octave note; the borrowed groove, as the square of the amount.
DrumsSEQ
How long the kick holds a shape (2 to 6 phrases) and how far it wanders; the shaker's steps trade places; the clap's odds.
NoiseSAVE
How often its groove is drawn anew at a phrase, rather than kept.
Form's alone
Now and then, at a chord change, the walk's other voicing.

3 · Drift HOME · Form KNOB 4

One amount for all the sound's slow movement; the notes, patterns and sections go on the same.
The LFOs
The chord's and the noise's, their wave and their wander: about an octave of the chord's filter at 50, 1.7 at 100.
LFO pagesENV x4, SAVE x3
Drift more or less of Form's (far left Still, +50 twice); Wave wander to wave; Rate 1 to 32 bars; Target Cutoff, Reso, Colour; Filter, Tone, Air (sheets 4, 8).
The sweeps
How far the filter's long sweeps go; the echo's heads, band-pass and feedback wandering.
The rides
Each part's level, echo and plate send, the chord's filter, the feedback: a new place every 10 to 60 s, any semicorchea (16th).at 100: levels -7..+3 dB, sends -8..+6, ±0.55 oct

4 · Motion's zones HOME · Form KNOB 3

The arrangement only. Each zone adds a kind of event to the ones before.
Breathes0 – 39
"Parts out and back, the kick out a bar or two": a part out (the chord, bass 12 dB down) or swelled into the echo, one a minute at 40; the kick out one in two minutes. 0: "Nothing leaves".
Sections40 – 69
"Thin and Groove phrases join, rarely to often": Thin (no bass, no shaker), Groove (every other stab), a phrase or two; 5% of the phrases at 40, 50% at 70. Fills join.
Breaks70 – 100
"Breaks join: the kick and the bass out a phrase", up to 60% of the departures at 100. Four phrases of Full between any two.

5 · Energy Form KNOB 1 · not saved

Moves
The pings' notes and level, Hits and the hats' level and colour, the stabs' count and length, the kick's shape, the bass's Density; most reach their end at ±50. No zones.
Leans
Past +25 a Break comes as a Groove; past -25 a Groove as a Thin. It never locks the sections.

How they stack

Patterns: Vary + each trim. The arrangement: Motion. The sound: Drift, each LFO's Drift. The harmony: Change, the walk. Now, no knob moved: Lock.

Most still

Vary 0 (or every trim Hold) · Motion 0 · Drift 0 · Change Hold · Kick Four · Lock on: a strict loop, nothing thrown.

Most restless

Vary 100, every trim +50 · Motion 100 · Drift 100, both LFOs' Drift +50 · Walk Free · Change 2 bars.
